FORT8 Privacy Policy

FORT8's call-blocking features run on your device.

1. Overview

FORT8 is a call-blocking app for Android. FORT8's call-blocking features run on your device, and FORT8 does not operate a server that stores your personal call data. This policy explains what information FORT8 processes, how it's used, and your choices.

2. Information We Process

To screen an incoming call, FORT8 processes the phone number and, when your carrier or Android supplies one, a caller display name. FORT8 does not access your call log, phone state, or text messages.

If you grant Contacts permission, FORT8 reads your saved contacts' names and numbers to identify callers and to support certain Premium features. FORT8 does not require this permission to use FORT8's core call-blocking features.

3. How We Use Information

FORT8 uses this information on your device to apply the blocking rules and allow-number exceptions you set up, and to decide whether to allow or block a call. When FORT8 blocks a call, it keeps a local record in your Blocked Call History, including the number, the time, and why the call was blocked. FORT8 also shows a simple notification when it blocks a call.

4. Local Storage and Retention

Your blocking rules, allow-number exceptions, schedules, and Blocked Call History are all stored locally on your device. FORT8 does not operate a central server or database that stores your call data, contacts, rules, or blocked-call history for any user.

Free: Blocked Call History is kept for 3 days, then automatically deleted. Premium: Blocked Call History is available without the 3-day retention limit.

5. Third-Party Services

Free users may see banner ads served by Google Mobile Ads. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be used to manage your advertising consent choices. Premium purchases are processed through Google Play Billing; FORT8 never receives or stores your payment details.

To verify Premium purchases and maintain Premium access, FORT8 securely sends limited purchase information, including the purchase token, app package name, and product ID, to a FORT8 server. The purchase information is used for purchase verification and Premium entitlement management.

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform are Google's own services. Google independently decides how these services process information, under Google's own privacy practices, which FORT8 does not control. The FORT8 server is used for Premium purchase verification and entitlement management.

6. Sharing and Security

FORT8 does not automatically share your call data, contacts, rules, or history with any third party. Premium users can export their Blocked Call History to a file. This only happens when they choose to start the export, and any sharing afterward uses Android's own sharing options and whatever destination they pick.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can delete individual rules within FORT8, revoke the Contacts permission at any time in Android's settings, and remove all of FORT8's locally stored data by clearing FORT8's app data or uninstalling FORT8.

8. Children's Privacy

FORT8 is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Third-party services FORT8 uses, such as Google's advertising services, may have their own independent data practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as FORT8 changes. This policy is effective as of 2026-09-26. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date in this section.
